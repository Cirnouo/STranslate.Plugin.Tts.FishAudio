# DD-060: Center voice action glyph outlines rather than font line boxes

**Status:** Current.

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23.

**Amends:** The icon alignment implementation in [DD-056](DD-056.md); selection and bookmark ownership remain active.

## Context

The voice card combines a vector star, a preview ring and font-based Play/Tick/Cross symbols. Equal button sizes and centered content presenters did not align their visible pixels. A supplied screenshot still showed the selection action above the star. The compiled fractional-DPI fixture reproduced the mismatch even though the earlier drawing-bounds assertions passed: glyph drawing bounds include black-box padding, while line boxes and independently rounded child layout do not describe actual visible outlines.

## Decision

Render the existing Segoe MDL2 Assets symbols through a small shared control that obtains their original glyph outlines, caches frozen geometry, and translates each outline's ink bounds to the content center. Preserve glyph shape, font size, native theme foreground, command, tooltip, accessibility name and existing button hit/focus behavior. Keep the two voice action rows from independently rounding child line boxes. The same helper serves the selected voice and the shared Search/By-ID/Favorites template.

Do not compensate with a hard-coded vertical offset for one scale or align only the outer click targets. Play, Tick and Cross retain their original symbols; the star, Stop square, spinner and preview ring remain their existing geometries.

## Consequences and verification

Outline geometry no longer depends on a text line's ascent/descent or padding. Physical raster tests still need to account for legitimate half-pixel edge quantization of different contours; a vector bounding box alone cannot establish perceived alignment. Preserve the known failing fractional-DPI case, exercise supported scale/text-formatting states, and check the installed host separately. Do not describe a geometric fixture as a listening or universal visual guarantee.
