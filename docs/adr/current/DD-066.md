# DD-066: Supervise regression partitions outside the native STA

Status: accepted for the current executable regression runner.

## Context

The previous twenty-second execution guard could reject a slow action that later completed its assertions and cleanup. Waiting on the same failed task added diagnostics, but could not recover a blocked Dispatcher. A whole suite can also legitimately exceed one action's protective limit because it owns many independent native workflows.

## Decision

Run the five catalog partitions in supervised child processes. Keep local native execution serial and selection finer than the process boundary. Preserve each partition's known first-use ordering and the legacy selectors' original delegates and arguments.

Separate a twenty-second slow-run warning from a 120-second hard guard. A root action's immutable monotonic deadline starts before queueing and includes cleanup; nested work cannot renew it. Startup uses separate bounded stages. Functional provider, input, dwell and fake-clock deadlines remain assertions of their original contracts.

At a hard failure, retain the original outcome and spend at most ten seconds on auxiliary diagnostics before terminating the polluted process from outside its STA. Drain both pipes independently of console output, do not retry or replace the action, and continue other selected partitions. Require exact child identity and complete terminal evidence before accepting success.

Publish immutable completion snapshots for live parent reads and compare the canonical final manifest only after confirmed child exit. A Windows pressure fixture reproduced sharing conflicts when repeated replacements raced live readers, including readers that allowed shared deletion. Immutable snapshot ownership removes that contention without retrying an action or accepting an older success.

## Consequences

Slow finite work can pass with a warning, while a stalled process cannot prevent other groups from providing evidence. Fresh process boundaries make cold behavior explicit and reduce shared native state between domains. Process startup adds a small cost; this decision alone does not claim a measured full-run improvement. Coverage and performance comparisons remain separate evidence requirements.

The [build specification](../../specs/build-and-release.md#regression-process-isolation) owns execution behavior and the [verification policy](../../policies/verification.md) owns contributor choices.
